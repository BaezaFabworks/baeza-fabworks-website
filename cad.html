3D printing is another tool in the Baeza Fabworks shop for solving problems that don’t always require cutting and welding metal.

Prototype. Test.
Refine.

Whether it’s a prototype, replacement component, bracket, fixture, adapter, template, or completely custom part, 3D printing makes it possible to turn an idea into something you can hold, test, fit, and refine.

Baeza Fabworks can print from customer-supplied STL or 3D model files, modify existing designs when practical, and create straightforward custom parts using CAD. More complex design work can be evaluated on a project-by-project basis.

For fabrication projects, printed parts can also be used as templates, fixtures, mockups, and design aids before moving into metal.

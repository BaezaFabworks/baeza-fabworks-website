Website Information

Privacy Notice.

How information submitted through the Baeza Fabworks website is used.

Information we collect

When you request a quote, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, project type, desired timeline, and details about your project. If you later email photos, drawings, measurements, STL files, or other project materials, those items are also information you choose to provide.

How we use it

Baeza Fabworks uses this information to review your request, communicate with you, prepare or discuss a quote, and provide requested services. We do not use the website quote form to collect payment-card, financial, medical, or other sensitive information.

Form processing

The website quote form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to Formspree so it can deliver the request to Baeza Fabworks. Formspree may process and retain submissions according to its own terms, privacy policy, security practices, and the account plan in use.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the Baeza Fabworks website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. The site uses browser session storage only to remember the visitor’s Enter the Shop and sound preferences during the browsing session. Third-party services used by the site may have their own technical logging or security practices.

Sharing and retention

Project information is shared only as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, operate the website, or provide the requested service. Information may also be retained when reasonably needed for project records, business records, legal obligations, or dispute resolution.

Your choices

You may contact Baeza Fabworks to ask a question about information you submitted or request that information be corrected or deleted when it is no longer reasonably needed or legally required.
